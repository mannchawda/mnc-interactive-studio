// ABOUT THE STUDIO

A Studio Built on
Craft & Code.

MNC. Interactive Studios is an independent creative studio specializing in game development, 3D art, and web experiences. We operate at the intersection of engineering precision and artistic vision — building things that are technically sound and visually unforgettable.

Founded by Mann Chawda, a Computer Science engineer and self-taught 3D artist with a deep passion for interactive media. Every project we take on is built from scratch, with full ownership of the craft — no templates, no shortcuts.
